The brief

What a website in this industry has to do, and how this one does it.

  1. Compare in your own currency

    Pilgrims abroad compare agencies on price, so every price can switch between USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD, and the choice is remembered.

  2. Three clear tiers

    Essentials, Signature and Elite, each with nights in Makkah and Madinah, the hotel, the walking distance to the Haram and what's included.

  3. Scholars build trust

    Travelling scholars with their credentials, licensing and years in business, all marked up as structured data.

  4. Dated departures

    Winter and Ramadan 2027 departures with seats, plus Hajj 2027 guidance as soon as registration opens.

(02)

Design direction

Makkah at night: midnight navy and champagne gold, with bronze for gold text on light backgrounds so it stays readable. Cormorant Garamond for display over Manrope for text, arch-topped photos and a fine gold eight-point star pattern.

The hero is a full-bleed video of the Haram at night with a large serif headline, an announcement bar for Ramadan and Hajj news, and a currency picker in the header.

Motion. Slow and luxurious: soft fades, gentle rises and a hero video that pauses for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

(06)

How it's built

  • One source file per page with front matter (path, title, description, nav) and shared partials, rendered by a custom build step.
  • JSON-LD is generated from what's visible on each page, so the structured data always matches the page.
  • Prices are stored once in a base currency and converted in the browser with Intl.NumberFormat; without JavaScript they still show in US dollars.
  • The hero video ships as WebM and MP4, muted, with a poster image.
  • Every page works without JavaScript, and visitors who ask for reduced motion get a calm, static page.

Rihla FAQ

Who designed and built the Rihla Hajj & Umrah website?

Shahin Alam, a full-stack software engineer in Dhaka, Bangladesh, designed and built the Rihla Hajj & Umrah website in 2026: the research, the design, the front-end code, the structured data and the search setup for all 15 pages.

What is the Rihla website built with?

Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with Motion for animation, generated into 15 static pages by a custom build step. There's no framework and no CMS, and every page works without JavaScript.

What are the main features of the Rihla website?

Its main features are: Currency switcher, Journey finder, Three Umrah tiers, Departures and Accessible hero video.

How is the Rihla website optimised for Google and AI search?

Every one of its 15 pages has a canonical URL, one h1, an Open Graph image and JSON-LD (TravelAgency · TouristTrip · Offer · Service · Person · FAQPage). 15 FAQ answers are marked up, the guides are written answer-first, and an llms.txt summary plus a robots.txt that welcomes 9 AI crawlers by name help assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity find and cite it.

Is there a related website to Rihla?

Yes. Al-Mabrur Hajj & Umrah serves the same industry for a different audience (Dhaka, Bangladesh, Bangla · prices in taka), with its own design.

Where can I see the Rihla website?

It's live at rihla.shahinkhan.online. It works on phones, tablets and desktops.

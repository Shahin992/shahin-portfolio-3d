About Shahin Alam

I'm a full-stack software engineer in Dhaka, Bangladesh. I turn complex business workflows into fast, reliable web applications.

In short Shahin Alam is a full-stack software engineer based in Mirpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh, with 2.5+ years of hands-on experience building scalable, high-performance web applications with React.js, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, Docker and RESTful APIs. He works as a Junior Software Developer at Orangetoolz, studied Computer Science & Engineering at Mirpur College, and was named Performer of the Quarter for Q3 2024.

I started my professional journey in January 2024 as a MERN stack developer intern at Core Devs in Mirpur DOHS, Dhaka. There I built scalable applications with React, Node.js and MongoDB, delivered key features for products including Gold Squid VIP, Whale Shawp and point-of-sale (POS) systems, optimized REST APIs and diagnosed critical bugs.

In March 2024 I joined Orangetoolz in Uttara, Dhaka as a Junior Software Developer. I engineer core CRM workflows: automated emailing systems, auto-dialer logic, user interaction tracking and data-processing pipelines for skip tracing, data cleaning and map scraping. I build full-stack features with React.js and Next.js, containerize microservices with Docker, and have integrated GPT-based AI features and services like Nylas, Zapier, NowCerts and EZLynx. In Q3 2024 I was named Performer of the Quarter.

Outside work I build my own products. EduFusion is a school management SaaS for Bangladesh that I founded in 2026: a React web app, a NestJS API, an Android app and a website, with AI-generated question papers and WhatsApp messaging for guardians. ColdMailer is a self-hosted cold-email platform with human-paced sending and a mailbox warm-up engine. I also built Cliento, a full-stack CRM with Stripe subscription billing and a Gmail-powered inbox, and I design and build marketing websites: eight multi-page sites in six industries, in English and Bangla.

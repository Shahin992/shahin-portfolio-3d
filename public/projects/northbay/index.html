The brief

What a website in this industry has to do, and how this one does it.

  1. Capacity, compliance, quality

    These carry the most weight in buyers' supplier scorecards, so: a capacity calendar, a capability matrix, certifications and quality details, with numbers.

  2. MOQ per style and colour

    Every product page states the minimum order per style and per colour, and the fabric minimum behind it.

  3. Lead times with a start

    Sampling and bulk lead times shown separately, with the clock starting at a named event, plus a time-and-action planner.

  4. Duty and traceability

    Duty-free access status, cotton origin, lot-level records and the US and EU forced-labour rules, covered in plain articles.

  5. Safety and green credentials

    Safety inspections, a LEED-rated building, rooftop solar and water recycling, each shown with figures.

  6. An RFQ that fits the trade

    Tech-pack upload, a 48-hour reply promise, an NDA on request and a gated compliance pack.

(02)

Design direction

A spec sheet. The page is laid on a technical-drawing grid with faint 12-column rules, crop marks on photos and dimension lines on section heads. The navigation is numbered in mono (01–06), the utility bar runs a live Dhaka clock, and the RFQ button is thread red.

IBM Plex Sans (with Condensed) and IBM Plex Mono, on white and paper grey with denim indigo and thread red. Figures are set in mono, in tables, because buyers read numbers.

Motion. Technical: lines draw in, counters tick and "stitch" dividers run across the page.

What it does

  1. Animated technical flats

    SVG flats of a tee, polo, hoodie, jogger, 5-pocket jean and kids' romper that draw themselves, with measurement callouts.

  2. Tech-pack card

    Tabs for four garments with style, fabric, composition, MOQ and a points-of-measure chart, right on the home page.

  3. Capacity calendar

    Knit and denim capacity booked by ex-factory month.

  4. Capability matrix

    What the factory can do, by product and process, at a glance.

  5. Time-and-action planner

    Pick a PO date and see every milestone to ex-factory and arrival port.

  6. Factory tour

    Nine stops from knitting and dyeing to packing, with figures for each.

  7. Six product pages

    MOQs, lead times, FOB price bands, fabrics and measurement charts per product.

  8. RFQ with tech pack

    Upload a tech pack and get a costed reply within 48 hours; the compliance pack is gated.

  9. Notes for sourcing teams

    Articles on duty-free access, cotton traceability and forced-labour rules.

(06)

How it's built

  • One products file drives the six product pages, the home-page tech-pack card and the product cards; the SVG flats, matrix, calendar and tour come from small data tables.
  • Every product carries Product, Offer and PriceSpecification data that matches the visible price bands.
  • A headless-Chrome interaction test clicks through the planner, matrix and tour, and a responsive audit loads every page at nine screen widths.
  • Every page works without JavaScript, and visitors who ask for reduced motion get a calm, static page.

Northbay FAQ

Who designed and built the Northbay Garments website?

Shahin Alam, a full-stack software engineer in Dhaka, Bangladesh, designed and built the Northbay Garments website in 2026: the research, the design, the front-end code, the structured data and the search setup for all 24 pages.

What is the Northbay website built with?

Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with Motion for animation, generated into 24 static pages by a custom build step. There's no framework and no CMS, and every page works without JavaScript.

What are the main features of the Northbay website?

Its main features are: Animated technical flats, Tech-pack card, Capacity calendar, Capability matrix and Time-and-action planner.

How is the Northbay website optimised for Google and AI search?

Every one of its 24 pages has a canonical URL, one h1, an Open Graph image and JSON-LD (Organization · Product · Offer · PriceSpecification · FAQPage · HowTo). 15 FAQ answers are marked up, the guides are written answer-first, and an llms.txt summary plus a robots.txt that welcomes 13 AI crawlers by name help assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity find and cite it.

Where can I see the Northbay website?

It's live at northbay.shahinkhan.online. It works on phones, tablets and desktops.

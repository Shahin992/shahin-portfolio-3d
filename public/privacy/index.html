Privacy & cookies

This site has no ads, no forms and no accounts. It uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. Here is exactly what that means.

Who is responsible

Shahin Alam, Dhaka, Bangladesh, runs this website. Questions about your data: shahin.khan.dev@gmail.com.

What Google Analytics collects

Google Analytics 4 records the pages you view, how you arrived (for example from a search or a link), your approximate location (country and city, worked out from your IP address, which Google Analytics 4 does not store), your device type, browser, screen size and language, and actions such as scrolling, clicking a link to another site or downloading the résumé.

Google Signals and ad personalisation are switched off, and there is no advertising on this site. The data is used only to understand which pages are useful.

Cookies

When analytics is allowed, Google Analytics sets two first-party cookies, _ga and _ga_T25P76EM9N, which expire after about 13 months. They tell repeat visits apart; they do not identify you by name.

Your choice

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics cookies are set only after you choose Accept in the banner. Elsewhere, analytics is on by default. Anyone can change their choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page; browser settings and content blockers also work.

Stored in your browser only

Your colour theme (light or dark) and your cookie choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They are never sent anywhere.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, which processes standard request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the site.

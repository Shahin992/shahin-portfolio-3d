The brief

What a website in this industry has to do, and how this one does it.

  1. Phone-first, app-like

    Most visitors arrive on a phone, so the site behaves like an app: a bottom tab bar (home, tours, visa, guides, call), a centre call button and a floating WhatsApp button.

  2. One search for everything

    Tours, visas, hotels and air tickets in one tabbed search in the hero, because a Bangladeshi agency usually sells all four.

  3. Prices people can act on

    Taka prices in Bangla digits on every card, and the booking rule up front: book with a 20% advance and pay the rest by bKash, Nagad or card.

  4. Rules that change

    Saint Martin's season, the Sundarbans closure, the Sajek escort and visa types are checked and dated on the page.

(02)

Design direction

A vibrant, app-like bento. A cream background, white surfaces and navy ink carry a coral primary with teal, sun, leaf and violet accents. Cards have 28px corners, buttons and chips are pills, and prices sit on rotated "sticker" badges.

Baloo Da 2 for headings and Hind Siliguri for text, self-hosted with Bengali and Latin subsets. The header has a segmented pill nav with a sliding active pill.

Motion. Springy and playful: bento tiles pop in, chips arrive staggered, stickers wobble, the tab indicator slides and cards lift on a spring.

What it does

  1. Tabbed search

    Accessible tabs for tours, visas, hotels and air tickets; the choices carry over to the listings as URL filters.

  2. Bento hero

    The headline and search card surrounded by photo tiles with price stickers, a stats tile and a rating tile.

  3. Trip-type chips

    Sea, hills, forest, tea gardens, islands, honeymoon, family and corporate, in a scrolling row.

  4. Tours from one data file

    Every tour's name, price, duration, image and highlights live in one file that feeds the cards, the listings and the trip pages.

  5. International tours

    A snap carousel of trips abroad with the visa included.

  6. Visa help

    Visa tiles with processing-time badges for the most-asked countries.

  7. Season calendar

    The best time to visit eight destinations, month by month.

  8. Four-step booking

    The whole booking process explained in four online steps.

  9. Guides & FAQ

    Cox's Bazar, Sajek costs and the Thailand visa, written answer-first.

SEO

Ranking in Google

Bangla titles for the searches people type, with the price in the title: "কক্সবাজার ট্যুর প্যাকেজ ৩ দিন ২ রাত", "সাজেক ভ্যালি ট্যুর প্যাকেজ", "থাইল্যান্ড ট্যুর প্যাকেজ". Every page declares lang="bn" and a bn-BD hreflang.

AEO

Answer-first content

Guides answer the question in their titles: Cox's Bazar costs and best time, what a Sajek trip costs, and the Thailand e-visa step by step. 19 FAQ answers come from one data file, so the visible answers and the schema always match.

GEO

Cited by AI assistants

TravelAgency and TouristTrip schema with Offer prices for every tour, a detailed llms.txt and 13 AI crawlers welcomed by name in robots.txt.

How it's built

  • Page front matter and shared includes keep the header, footer and tour cards identical on all 17 pages, and the JSON-LD is built from what each page shows.
  • One tours.json feeds the cards, the listings and the TouristTrip data; one faqs.json feeds both the visible answers and the FAQPage schema, and a QA check confirms they match.
  • Bengali and Latin font subsets are self-hosted, and the two critical files are preloaded so Bangla text never flashes in a fallback font.
  • Every page works without JavaScript, and visitors who ask for reduced motion get a calm, static page.

Swapnojatra FAQ

Who designed and built the Swapnojatra Tours website?

Shahin Alam, a full-stack software engineer in Dhaka, Bangladesh, designed and built the Swapnojatra Tours website in 2026: the research, the design, the front-end code, the structured data and the search setup for all 17 pages.

What is the Swapnojatra website built with?

Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with Motion for animation, generated into 17 static pages by a custom build step. There's no framework and no CMS, and every page works without JavaScript.

What are the main features of the Swapnojatra website?

Its main features are: Tabbed search, Bento hero, Trip-type chips, Tours from one data file and International tours.

How is the Swapnojatra website optimised for Google and AI search?

Every one of its 17 pages has a canonical URL, one h1, an Open Graph image and JSON-LD (TravelAgency · TouristTrip · Offer · Service · FAQPage · HowTo). 19 FAQ answers are marked up, the guides are written answer-first, and an llms.txt summary plus a robots.txt that welcomes 13 AI crawlers by name help assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity find and cite it.

Is there a related website to Swapnojatra?

Yes. Fernway Journeys serves the same industry for a different audience (Worldwide, English · USD), with its own design.

Where can I see the Swapnojatra website?

It's live at swapnojatra.shahinkhan.online. It works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Questions, answered

Who is Shahin Alam?

Shahin Alam is a full-stack software engineer based in Mirpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh, with 2.5+ years of hands-on experience. He builds scalable, high-performance web applications with React.js, Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript, Docker and RESTful APIs, and works as a Junior Software Developer at Orangetoolz, where he was named Performer of the Quarter for Q3 2024.

What technologies does Shahin Alam work with?

His core stack is React.js, Next.js, TypeScript and JavaScript (ES6+), with Redux Toolkit, RTK Query, React Query and Zustand for state. On the backend he uses Node.js, Express.js, NestJS, Prisma, PostgreSQL and MongoDB, along with JWT, Google OAuth, Docker and Firebase. See the full skills page.

What kind of projects has Shahin Alam built?

Mostly CRM platforms and business tools: automated email systems, auto-dialer logic, user interaction tracking, data pipelines (skip tracing, data cleaning, map scraping), GPT-powered content features, Docker-containerized microservices and integrations with Nylas, Zapier, NowCerts, EZLynx, Stripe and the Gmail API. His own products include EduFusion, a school management SaaS for Bangladesh, and ColdMailer, a self-hosted cold-email platform. He also built Cliento, a full-stack CRM, and eight multi-page websites in six industries (HVAC, therapy, kitchens, garments, travel, Hajj and Umrah), in English and Bangla, each live on a shahinkhan.online subdomain.

Is Shahin Alam a frontend or a full-stack developer?

Full-stack. He builds React and Next.js frontends and Node.js back ends with Express.js and NestJS, containerizes microservices with Docker, works with MongoDB and PostgreSQL, and has shipped complete MERN applications end to end, including the frontend and backend of Cliento.

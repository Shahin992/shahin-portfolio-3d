The brief

What a website in this industry has to do, and how this one does it.

  1. Trust comes first

    Pilgrims worry about fraud, so licensing, association membership, years in business and pilgrims served sit in the first screen, as the strongest agency sites do.

  2. The package card is the product

    Tier, from-price, days, hotel star rating and walking distance to the Haram, flights and visa, on every card.

  3. WhatsApp converts

    In Bangladesh enquiries happen on WhatsApp, so there's a floating button, a header button, a pre-filled message on every package and a mobile call and WhatsApp bar.

  4. Education builds authority

    Umrah step by step, a documents checklist, Hajj 2027 registration, Makkah hotels by distance and Umrah for women: content that ranks and gets quoted by AI.

  5. Beat both kinds of competitor

    The local leader had strong answer-first content but plain visuals; the premium sites had visuals but weak structured data. Al-Mabrur combines both.

(02)

Design direction

Calm, sacred and premium, drawn from Islamic architecture: mihrab arches frame the photos, an eight-point star pattern runs through the backgrounds, and the palette takes the green of the Prophet's Mosque dome and the gold of the Kaaba's calligraphy.

Headings in Noto Serif Bengali and text in Hind Siliguri, with Amiri for Arabic phrases and Fraunces for Latin text. The hero is a muted video loop with a framed arch beside the headline.

Motion. Gentle and reverent: soft fades and rises, counters that roll up in Bangla digits, and nothing that bounces.

What it does

  1. Package tabs

    Umrah, Hajj 2027 and Ramadan tabs, with four tiers each: hotel stars and distance in Makkah and Madinah, inclusions and the from-price.

  2. WhatsApp package finder

    Choose the package, month, number of travellers and budget, and it opens WhatsApp with the enquiry written for you.

  3. Quick answer box

    A dated, answer-first paragraph on what Umrah costs from Bangladesh in 2027, written for featured snippets and AI answers.

  4. Booking in six steps

    From the first consultation to the flight, as numbered steps.

  5. Guides

    Hajj 2027 registration, Makkah hotels by distance and Umrah for women, plus a full Umrah guide.

  6. Mobile call bar

    Call and WhatsApp buttons fixed to the bottom of every phone screen.

How it's built

  • Pages are generated from shared partials and sections by a custom build step, which also writes the structured data, sitemap.xml and llms.txt.
  • Copy is written with ASCII digits, and the build turns visible numbers into Bangla digits (৳1,35,000 → ৳১,৩৫,০০০) while leaving data attributes, links and flight or licence codes alone. Counters and the footer year use bn-BD formatting in JavaScript.
  • A Bangla style guide fixes the spelling of terms like হজ and ওমরাহ across every page.
  • Bengali font subsets are self-hosted, with the two critical files preloaded; the hero video ships as WebM and MP4.
  • Every page works without JavaScript, and visitors who ask for reduced motion get a calm, static page.

Al-Mabrur FAQ

Who designed and built the Al-Mabrur Hajj & Umrah website?

Shahin Alam, a full-stack software engineer in Dhaka, Bangladesh, designed and built the Al-Mabrur Hajj & Umrah website in 2026: the research, the design, the front-end code, the structured data and the search setup for all 14 pages.

What is the Al-Mabrur website built with?

Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with Motion for animation, generated into 14 static pages by a custom build step. There's no framework and no CMS, and every page works without JavaScript.

What are the main features of the Al-Mabrur website?

Its main features are: Package tabs, WhatsApp package finder, Quick answer box, Booking in six steps and Guides.

How is the Al-Mabrur website optimised for Google and AI search?

Every one of its 14 pages has a canonical URL, one h1, an Open Graph image and JSON-LD (TravelAgency · TouristTrip · Offer · OfferCatalog · Service · HowTo · FAQPage). 15 FAQ answers are marked up, the guides are written answer-first, and an llms.txt summary plus a robots.txt that welcomes 8 AI crawlers by name help assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity find and cite it.

Yes. Rihla Hajj & Umrah serves the same industry for a different audience (UK, USA, Canada & Australia, English · 5 currencies), with its own design.

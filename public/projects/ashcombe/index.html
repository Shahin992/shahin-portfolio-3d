The brief

What a website in this industry has to do, and how this one does it.

  1. Proof in photos first

    Buyers want to see finished rooms, sorted by style, before they get in touch: a filterable masonry gallery, a project rail and nine project stories with the brief, the materials and the cost.

  2. Price signals before the visit

    "Most of our kitchens are £38,000–£85,000", with clear includes and excludes, a cost estimator and project figures.

  3. A free first meeting

    A free first consultation in the showroom, at home or by video, then a design fee that's deducted from the order.

  4. Honest lead times

    A live "next workshop slot" line and a stage-by-stage timeline: five stages, about five months.

  5. Fair deposits

    25% on order, 65% two weeks before delivery and 10% on completion, with plain-English cancellation terms.

  6. UK legal basics

    Company and VAT details on every page, a privacy notice, and a cookie banner where "Reject all" is as prominent as "Accept all". Nothing optional loads before consent, not even the map.

(02)

Design direction

An editorial gallery, like a monograph on a coffee table. The wordmark sits in the centre with links split either side, and a full-height overlay menu shows a photo preview. A 12-column grid places photos off-centre with "plate" numbers and wall-label captions, square corners and hairline rules.

Bodoni Moda, with its high-contrast italics, for display and Jost for text, on limewash, charcoal, oak and brass.

Motion. Unhurried: curtain wipes reveal the photos, headings rise line by line behind masks, and the project rail drags with momentum.

What it does

  1. Masonry gallery

    Filter finished rooms by room, collection and finish, then open them in a lightbox.

  2. Sketch-to-installed sliders

    Drag between a pencil drawing and the finished room.

  3. Swatch library

    20 colours, 8 timbers and 9 stones, with a sample-box builder.

  4. Cost estimator

    A rough budget for your room before the first meeting.

  5. Consultation booking

    Showroom, home or video, with a booking calendar.

  6. Nine project stories

    The brief, the materials, the cost and the timeline for each finished room.

  7. Consent-first cookies

    "Reject all" is as easy as "Accept all", and Google Maps only loads after consent or a click.

  8. Two showrooms

    Bristol and Battersea pages with hours, workshop tours and maps.

  9. Trade & brochure

    A page for architects and interior designers, a printed-brochure request and aftercare.

How it's built

  • Data files drive the repeated parts: nine project stories, the gallery, the rail and the price links from one file; six collection pages from another; the swatch library from a third.
  • The image pipeline crops every photo from its focal point and also draws the pencil "designer's drawings" for the sliders.
  • A headless-Chrome responsive audit loads every page at nine screen widths, on top of a static QA pass.
  • Every page works without JavaScript, and visitors who ask for reduced motion get a calm, static page.

Ashcombe & Hale FAQ

Who designed and built the Ashcombe & Hale website?

Shahin Alam, a full-stack software engineer in Dhaka, Bangladesh, designed and built the Ashcombe & Hale website in 2026: the research, the design, the front-end code, the structured data and the search setup for all 36 pages.

What is the Ashcombe & Hale website built with?

Hand-written HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript with Motion for animation, generated into 36 static pages by a custom build step. There's no framework and no CMS, and every page works without JavaScript.

What are the main features of the Ashcombe & Hale website?

Its main features are: Masonry gallery, Sketch-to-installed sliders, Swatch library, Cost estimator and Consultation booking.

How is the Ashcombe & Hale website optimised for Google and AI search?

Every one of its 36 pages has a canonical URL, one h1, an Open Graph image and JSON-LD (FurnitureStore · Service · Offer · OfferCatalog · FAQPage · HowTo). 33 FAQ answers are marked up, the guides are written answer-first, and an llms.txt summary plus a robots.txt that welcomes 13 AI crawlers by name help assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity find and cite it.

Where can I see the Ashcombe & Hale website?

It's live at ashcombe.shahinkhan.online. It works on phones, tablets and desktops.
